Grown-up corner

A steady paper surface

Lock paper is on by default. It fixes the desk in place and blocks page panning on the practice surface, including palm touches outside the canvas. The guide chooser and this panel can still scroll. Uncheck Lock paper when you need a scrollable page.

Pencil only ignores finger ink. Browser pen input is not native PencilKit palm rejection; real iPad and Apple Pencil behavior still needs device testing. For longer lines, landscape gives your child more writing room.

Make your own practice line

Guidance, not grading

Follow the dots gives generous path-based feedback for each stroke. My own try shows the words above a blank lined pad, and the child or parent marks an attempt. Neither mode recognizes handwriting or assesses reading or writing ability. A school may teach a different print formation.
