Grown-up corner · Pencil, guidance & saves

Use an Apple Pencil paired with a compatible iPad and open this page in Safari. Turn on Pencil only to ignore touch marks on the pad; buttons still accept fingers. This uses browser pen events, not PencilKit or Scribble. Actual Pencil behavior depends on your device and browser and has not been physically verified for this release.

Guidance, not grading. Follow the dots checks proximity to a path, start, direction and coverage with generous tolerances. It is not handwriting recognition or proof of skill. My own try has no automatic assessment: the child or grown-up marks an attempt. These original print guides use one consistent formation style, including single-storey a and g; your school may teach a different stroke order. Stop for a break whenever your child is ready.

Hear the guide uses the device’s English voice, not a new natural voice recording. Show me is optional and respects Reduce Motion: with it enabled, a complete stroke is shown without movement.

Privacy and progress. There is no microphone, login, handwriting upload, paid API or analytics. Only a list of practice badges and settings is saved locally. Ink is kept in memory during this visit and is not retained after closing or refreshing. Save a picture below to keep a page. Writing blooms do not spend or award kingdom coins. Kingdom backups and writing badges are separate in this first release.
