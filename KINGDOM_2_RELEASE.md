# Kingdom Garden Junior 2.0 — Living City & Unified Rewards

## What is implemented
- **Shared Sunshine Coins, preserving existing saves.** Existing `kgj-kingdom-v1` records, purchased vehicles, land, farm and flowers remain in place.
- Every completed new book: **10 coins**. Another completed reading of the same book on the same day: **3 coins**; later-day rereads: **5 coins**. Each reading requires all story pages in a fresh recorded session; reopening a completion screen does not reissue rewards.
- Every completed guided handwriting attempt or multiword line: **4 coins**. Every completed five-question math game: **7 coins**, including assisted practice. Rewards write into the exact same Kingdom Coins wallet.
- All writing/math reward events carry unique session identifiers and are deduplicated by the Kingdom Core action reducer. Completion coins survive reloading when browser storage is available.
- **14 vehicles** now available, from the 20-coin Little Red Car to the **500-coin Giant Delivery Truck**. Cars have different colors and visual details.
- **9 land/building upgrades** in the shop, including playground, bakery, village, orchard, fountain and storybook castle.
- New **[My Wonderful City](city.html)** page with a responsive illustrated map, a moving selected vehicle following a curved road, destination buttons, a garage, a vehicle shop, purchase confirmations, and displayed owned cars. Acquired landmarks appear in the city.
- Main reading app contains prominent City 2.0 links; existing reading, writing, math, kingdom farm and audio pages remain accessible.

## Safety and learning
All coins are fictional, earned by completing activities, and cannot be bought. No advertisements, fuel fees, timed losses, accounts, analytics or child data upload are added. Feedback is supportive and does not claim to assess academic mastery.

## Important limits
- This is the first **playable city release**, not a fully editable 3D simulation. Cars travel along a planned road, with tap-to-destination navigation. Buildings appear when owned; arbitrary object placement and wandering characters remain later roadmap items.
- City art is original lightweight SVG/CSS, rather than individually AI-painted or true 3D assets.
- Math/writing rewards apply to new completed sessions after this version. Existing reading/kingdom wallet balances are not reset. Existing writing/math badges remain intact.
- The existing device speech/narration fallback remains, and human-recorded narration was not generated by this release.
- Every user device saves locally. Browser deletion will remove local data; use the existing Grown-up Corner save export to back up kingdom purchases.

The old `kingdom-core-v1.js` remains in the repository. Existing legacy v1 user data is still stored under `kgj-kingdom-v1`, with recovery snapshots. Restore prior markup and script versions through standard GitHub revert (never force push).
